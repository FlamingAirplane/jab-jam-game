# Look

How the game looks and sounds. References help Claude more than adjectives.

## Mood

*Three or four words for how it should feel.*

Example: "Foggy, quiet, lonely, warm light."

> Dark fantasy mosaic. Cold, vast, moonlit, overwhelming — you are small and the dungeon is not.

## Palette

*Colors, or a description of them.*

Example: "Grey-blue sea, near-black sky, one warm yellow for the beam."

> Five greys for the whole world: #595959, #7f7f7f, #a5a5a5, #cccccc, #f2f2f2. Nothing in the moon
> dungeon has a colour of its own — the only colour on screen is the elements. Each element gets one.

## References

*Games, films, art, or photos that capture it. Put images and links in `context/references/` and say here what each one is for.*

Example: "The fog in *Limbo*; Hopper's lighthouse paintings."

> Dark fantasy built in a **mosaic art style** — that's the whole look of the floors and walls.
> *Diablo*, *Halls of Torment*, *Vampire Survivors* and *Infamous* for how the combat should feel.
> Images live in our own content package; we'll drop the best ones into `context/references/`.
