# Concept

Your game, in your words. Claude builds from this file, so what you write here steers everything.
Examples come from a different imaginary game. Don't copy them; they show the size of an answer.

## Pitch

*In one or two sentences, what is the game?*

Example: "You are a lighthouse keeper guiding ships through fog by sweeping a beam across the sea."

> An action RPG game that you can kill some time with. You just play it for a little bit, you get
> stronger, and then you leave when you're bored. Then you come back to it — it's kind of like a
> daily routine.
>
> You're a golem in a dungeon on the moon. Humans built portals to get there; the dungeon has the
> resources humanity needs to reach the other planets. The golem absorbs the elements it finds in
> the environment, but it can only hold one at a time.

## Player fantasy

*What should the player feel like, or get to be?*

Example: "Calm but responsible: the only light in a dark storm."

> Beating level after level, barely winning by the skin of your teeth, but still constantly
> progressing against overwhelming odds.

## Why it's yours

*Where did this idea come from? What about it do you care about?*

Example: "My grandfather fished off the coast and talked about fog more than storms."

> It was made as a response to not knowing what the character would be, and us trying to make a
> unique character and a unique setting to make us stand apart.
